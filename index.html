ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Understanding BSEMC

The Bachelor of Science in Entertainment and Multimedia Computing (BSEMC) is a degree program that focuses on the development of digital entertainment and multimedia technologies. It combines creativity, artistic skills, and technical knowledge to produce interactive and engaging digital experiences.

The program introduces students to different fields, including game development, digital animation, and multimedia computing. It allows learners to explore how technology can be used to create games, animations, digital content, and other forms of interactive media.

BSEMC is an opportunity for creative individuals to develop their ideas, improve their technical abilities, and prepare for careers in the growing entertainment and multimedia industry.
